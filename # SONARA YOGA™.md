# Executive Summary

Sonara Yoga is a premium wellness platform that delivers highly personalised yoga experiences supported by curated music, expert coaching, women's health services and transformational retreats.

Unlike traditional yoga memberships, Sonara adapts to each user's:

- Goals
- Life stage
- Mood
- Stress levels
- Sleep quality
- Fitness level
- Music preferences

The platform evolves into a personal wellness concierge.
